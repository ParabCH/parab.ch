Our lecture series autumn 2026

This lecture series introduces current research and developments in the field of psychedelic science, with a focus on their clinical applications and pharmacological mechanisms. Invited speakers will present recent insights from preclinical and clinical research. The topics cover the neurobiology and pharmacology of psychedelics, their therapeutic applications in psychiatry, current clinical research, relevant chemical perspectives, and their historical and legal context.
